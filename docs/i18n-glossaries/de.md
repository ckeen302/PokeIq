# PokéIQ de-DE glossary (German review)

Register: du (lowercase du/dein/dir everywhere; capitalised only at a sentence start, incl. after a colon that opens a full sentence); ihr for "both of you / you all" (ihr beide, drückt).
Quotes: „…“. Ellipsis: " …" (space before, as in „Knapp daneben …“). Apostrophe: ’ (geht’s, hat’s).
Numbers: formatted by code; percent "{n} %". "#{n}" in UI → "Nr. {n}" (rank → "Platz {n}"); share texts (copied to social media) may keep "#".
