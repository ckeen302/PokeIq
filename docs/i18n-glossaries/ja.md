# PokéIQ ja glossary (Japanese review)

Register: UI statements, errors, help and FAQ in です・ます. Calls to action and taglines may use the volitional
「〜しよう」 (as official Pokémon copy does); share texts / OG taglines / quiz questions may be casual (「勝てるかな？」).
Don't mix registers inside one sentence (「〜いませんが、〜少なかった。」→「〜少なかったです。」). Personality-quiz items
(which-pokemon-quiz) are first-person plain statements; synopses/long-form content are plain だ・である form.
Game-quote parodies (404 page, 「こうかはばつぐんだ！」) stay plain, without the games' half-width spaces.

Typography: full-width 、。！？「」『』（） in Japanese text; 「」 for quotes and our own names, 『』 for game/film titles.
No spaces between Japanese words; no space between a brand (PokéIQ, PokéGuessr, Pokédle, Showdown, GeoGuessr…) and
Japanese (「PokéGuessrの記録」). Spaces kept around "vs", around units in formulas ("{v} m/s"), and in official titles
that have them (『Pokémon LEGENDS アルセウス』, 『New ポケモンスナップ』). Arabic half-width digits only (no １０). No
half-width katakana. Numbers formatted by code.
