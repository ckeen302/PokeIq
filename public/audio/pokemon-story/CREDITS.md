# The Story of Pokémon: music credits

The music on /lab/pokemon-story (and in the videos it makes) is four
original tracks released under CC0 on OpenGameArt.org. None of it is music
from, or a cover or remix of, any Pokémon game. Each licence was checked on
the track's page when it was added (November 2026). The film plays the
tracks together as layers and mixes them per chapter: a warm piano bed
throughout, a chiptune sparkle for the Game Boy years, a light rhythmic
layer for the busy chapters, and an orchestral swell for each chapter's big
moment. The whooshes, chimes, booms, crowd roars and link-cable blips are
synthesised in the browser.
