# Race sound credits

The crowd on /pokedex/race (and in the videos it makes) is built from CC0
recordings on OpenGameArt.org. Each licence was checked on the sound's page
when it was added (November 2026). Everything else (the starter's gun,
footfalls, the crowd's roar, the stadium's reverb and the timing beep) is
synthesised in the browser.
