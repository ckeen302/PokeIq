# Battle music credits

PokéIQ's battle music is original chiptune by independent composers, released
under permissive licences on OpenGameArt.org. None of it is music from, or a
cover or remix of, any Pokémon game. Each licence was checked on the track's
page when it was added (October 2026).

Every file here was converted to mono Ogg Vorbis (with an MP3 fallback) at a
lower bitrate to keep downloads small.
