# Mass Battle sound credits

The colosseum's crowd on /lab/mass-battle (and in the videos it makes) is a
CC0 recording from OpenGameArt.org. Its licence was checked on the sound's
page when it was added (November 2026). The music is the CC0 track Pokémon
Sizes uses (see `public/audio/sizes/CREDITS.md`). Everything else (the hits,
the recall beams, the booms, the wind on the plains and the snowfield, the
volcano's rumble) is synthesised in the browser.
