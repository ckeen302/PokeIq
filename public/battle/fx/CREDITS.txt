Battle effect images

The .png and .jpg images here are from Pokémon Showdown's client
(play.pokemonshowdown.com/fx/), which dedicates them to the public domain
(CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The images there that are not CC0 (icicle, icicle-pink, lightning, rocks,
rock1, rock2 and bone) are not used. The .svg files with those names were
drawn for PokéIQ instead.

The move animations that use these images are ported from Pokémon
Showdown's battle-animations-moves.ts (CC0) and battle-animations.ts (MIT,
Copyright (c) 2011-2026 Guangcong Luo and other contributors).
